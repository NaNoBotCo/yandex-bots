The Crawler Went Quiet

Drones struck Yandex data centers in Sasovo, Kaluga and Vladimir between 8 and 11 October 2026. Automated traffic from Yandex's network, as Cloudflare counts it, fell 93% in a day. Automated traffic from the rest of Russia barely moved: +0.1%.

Drag across the chart. Tap the spider.

Hour by hour

1 Oct 05:00 – 2 Oct 06:00 UTC. A week before the first strike, Yandex's bot traffic sat near 40 for 26 hours. No strike was reported.

8 Oct, 08:00 UTC. Sasovo, Ryazan region, overnight. Yandex's bot traffic steps down 34% within the hour and holds there.

9 Oct. Kaluga. Reported struck; the series shows no step that day.

11 Oct, 00:00–02:00 UTC. Vladimir. Yandex said operations at the facility were suspended. Bot traffic goes under 1% of its three-week peak from 01:00 to 13:00 UTC, flickers back to about a fifth of normal at 15:00, and settles near a tenth.

Human traffic from the same network fell 90% on 11 Oct. The whole network went dark, not the bots alone.

Yandex's share of Russia's bots

Of all automated requests from Russian networks, Yandex sent 9.9% on 4 October and 0.7% on 11 October. It had been the second-largest sender every day since 28 September; on 11 October it was 23rd.

Where Russia's 10% went

Russia's bot traffic on 4 October = 100. Each network's loss = its share that day × how far it fell.

Yandex: 9.87 × −93.1% = −9.19
Yandex Cloud: 1.19 × −79.8% = −0.95
All of Russia: 100 × −10.1% = −10.09
Everyone else: −10.09 − (−9.19) − (−0.95) = +0.05 on a base of 88.94 → +0.1%

Which bots

Radar sorts each request reaching a Cloudflare-served site as likely human or likely automated. It counts requests, not operators or purposes.

Before the strikes, 99.6% of Yandex's requests were automated. Yandex runs a search crawler that indexes the web; Radar's per-network counts don't say which Yandex service sent a request.

A request from a server outside Russia counts under that server's country. Bots run from foreign servers, home connections or phones don't show up as Yandex.

So the drop measures Yandex's own machines going offline. Whether any influence operation slowed is a separate question this data can't answer.
